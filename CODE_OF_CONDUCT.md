# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Contributors should be constructive, assume good intent, respect different
backgrounds and experience levels, and keep technical disagreement focused on
the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community. Report
conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
security reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as
practical.
